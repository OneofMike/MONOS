● THE IDEA
FIELD//01

THIS IS THE IDEA BEHIND WHAT I DO.

You bring me something messy, unclear, scattered, repetitive, difficult to compare, or hard to keep track of. I research it, organize it, and figure out what should exist around your specific situation.

Sometimes that becomes a dashboard. Sometimes it’s research, a comparison system, a tracker, an analysis, a planning tool, an interactive workspace, or something completely different.

I don’t start with the answer. I start with the situation.
● HOW I WORK
FIELD//02

UNDERSTAND → RESEARCH → ORGANIZE → BUILD

I first understand what you’re actually trying to accomplish, what’s getting in the way, what information already exists, and how you need to use it.

From there, I research what’s missing, organize the important pieces, and build the solution around the way you actually operate.

There’s no predetermined format you have to fit into. What gets built depends on what would actually be useful.
