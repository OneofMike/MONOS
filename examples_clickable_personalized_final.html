Andre was trying to choose his next performance car without buying something that looked good on paper but became expensive to own. He was concerned about mileage, reliability, hidden repair risks, maintenance costs, AWD, and whether each car was actually worth the money. He needed all of that research organized into one place so he could compare the cars clearly before buying.
